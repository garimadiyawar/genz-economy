Methodology

The Gen Z Survival Score is a weighted composite of five normalized dimensions. Each is min-max normalized to 0–100 across the observed historical range.

25%
Cost Burden
Rent-to-income ratio. Source: Zillow ZORI ÷ BLS median earnings 25–34. Inverted: higher burden = lower score.
25%
Employment Quality
Weighted blend: 40% unemployment rate, 30% U-6 underemployment, 30% employment-population ratio (ages 25–34).
20%
Real Wages
BLS median weekly earnings deflated by CPI-U. Captures whether purchasing power is growing or shrinking.
15%
Housing Access
Months of median income required to save 20% down payment at 10% savings rate. FRED MSPUS home price series.
15%
Education Debt
FRED total student loans outstanding ($B). Inverted. Proxy for systemic debt burden; Gen Z-specific data sparse.

About this report

This research report was produced using 100% free, publicly available government and research data. All analysis was performed in Python using DuckDB, pandas, and scikit-learn. Visualizations rendered with Chart.js. The data pipeline is open-source and designed for automated weekly refresh.

The Survival Score is a diagnostic tool, not a judgment. Scores below 50 reflect structural economic conditions — not personal failures of Gen Z individuals.

Data sources
